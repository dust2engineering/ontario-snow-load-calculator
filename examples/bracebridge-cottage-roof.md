# Example 2 — Bracebridge (Muskoka) roof, 6 m wide

SB-1 Bracebridge: Ss 3.1 kPa, Sr 0.4 kPa.
Part 9: S = 0.55 x 3.1 + 0.4 = 2.11 kPa — more than double the Toronto minimum.
Part 4 simplified: S = 3.1 x 0.8 + 0.4 = 2.88 kPa before drift factors.
This is why a prefab structure rated for the GTA cannot simply be dropped in cottage country.

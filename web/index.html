Ontario snow design starts from Supplementary Standard SB-1: a 1-in-50-year ground snow load Ss and a rain load Sr for each municipality. Part 9 turns them into a roof load with S = Cb x Ss + Sr (Cb 0.45 for roofs not wider than 4.3 m, otherwise 0.55, never less than 1.0 kPa). Part 4 uses S = Is [Ss (Cb Cw Ce Ca) + Sr]; this tool shows the uniform-load case with Cb 0.8 and Cw = Ce = Ca = 1.0, before drift and slope adjustments.
